Navigate

Every track on this site, step by step on your phone.

Open a track → ▶ Navigate
01
Follow the blue lineBlue = still to ride, grey = done. The way ahead is always up. Before each junction: which way, and how far. Two beeps at 200 m.
02
Tap the time boxArrival time, km to go, and when it gets dark.
03
⏺ Records by itselfNavigate records your GPX automatically, from start to stop. Stop NAV to save it.
04
Green line = your phoneIt points where your phone is facing, all the way up the screen. Turn the phone to look around. Faint line = the phone isn't sure which way it faces: ignore it and keep riding. It comes back bright once you ride faster.
05
🔒 Rain lockRain and gloves can't press anything. Drag to unlock: pull the tab on the left into the circle in the middle and hold it there 1 second.
06
🚶 WalkGetting off the bike to scout on foot? Tap 🚶 first: it pins your bike. Find my bike walks you back.
07
Offline mapOn the track page, tap while you have signal. The map then works with no signal.
The offline map saves by itself when you start Navigate. Start it while you still have signal. ■ NAV: stop, or ↩ ride back the way you came.
