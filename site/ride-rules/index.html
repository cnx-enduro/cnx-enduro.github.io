Group ride rules

Chiang Mai Enduro Riders

Every person joining a ride organized in the Chiang Mai Enduro Riders group agrees to these rules.

Disclaimer

A group of friendsChiang Mai Enduro Riders is only a group of friends who ride together — not a business, organisation or guide service.
No money and no servicesNo professional services are provided in the group and no money changes hands.
Your safety is on youRide leaders, group admins and other riders are not responsible for your safety or for any legal consequences of the rides.
No guaranteesNo GPS track is represented by us as safe or legal. Group information is recreational only, not safety or legal advice.
Enduro riding is dangerous: you join our rides at your own risk.

The golden rule: ride your own pace

The group adjusts to your speed, not the other way round.
Ride within your abilityDo not try to keep up with the group beyond your skill level.
Too difficult?Slow down, take the easier line, walk the bike, or ask for help. We’ve all been there.

Before the ride: arrive ready

Meeting pointBe on time10–15 min late is OK, longer holds up the group
Meeting pointFull tankFill up at the meeting point.
Meeting pointWater and snacksCarry at least 3 L of water and snacks

Full protective gear, always

🪖Helmet and goggles
🥾High boots
🧤Gloves
🦵Knee guards
🦺Chest protection

Before the ride: your bike

CRF or lighterHeavier bikes only for very experienced off-road riders
Check your bike
  • No leaks
  • Brakes working
  • Chain tension correct: a loose chain can come off and break your engine
Must have
  • Hand guards (save your levers when you crash)
  • Off-road tyres (50/50, or full enduro for L3-L4)
  • Basic tools
  • First aid kit
Recommended: rim locks · pull strap · tow line

Never pass the lead
Keep the leader's pace
Never fall behind the sweep
If you stop, the sweep stops with you

Help the rider in front

Look out for the buddy in front
  • They fall? Help lift the bike
  • Stuck on a hard section? Help push
  • Need more hands? Call the riders behind you
Don't lose the rider behind youOut of sight? Slow down until they catch up.

Speak up

Fatigue causes most crashes.
Pace or terrain too much?Tell the lead — they'll adjust.
Tired, hurt or out of your depth?Say so.
Slowing down or ending early is fine.

Spacing and stopping

Keep a safe gap — leave room to stop if the rider ahead falls.
More gapOn descents, in mud and in dust.
No tailgatingNever ride on someone's rear wheel.
Stopping?Pull fully off the line — never on a blind corner or behind a crest.

“Corner man” signal

Before every junction — given on the move: no stopping, no turning round.

01 · LeadTwo fingers to the goggles“Eyes on…” Left hand, keep riding.
02 · LeadTwo fingers up“…watch where I go.” Then the lead takes the turn.
03 · Rider behind the leadThumbs up“Got it, I’ll stop here.” Not passed down the line.
No thumbs-up? The lead slows down and repeats the signal, and stops if needed.

“Corner man” step by step

01SignalLead gives the V. Rider 2 gives a thumbs up.
02Stop and pointPark off the line. Point the way, arm level.
03Wait for sweepLet every rider pass. Stay until the sweep.
04RejoinRide on in front of the sweep.
Next junction: the new rider behind the lead becomes the corner man.

Hand signals

Hazard on that sideArm pointing down, left or right (rock, hole, root)
Slow downPalm down, waving up and down
StoppingArm raised, palm open
Rider needs help / group should stopHand tapping top of helmet
Pass every signal down the line to the rider behind you.

Respect the locals

Ride gently
  • ALWAYS slow down near villages, farms, people and animals. Walking pace, low throttle, low noise
  • Leave gates as you found them
  • No littering: pack out everything you bring in
Be friendly, never argue
  • Wave, nod, and smile
  • Never argue with locals or rangers. If they confront you, stay polite, apologise and leave.
Their goodwill keeps our trails open

Team play, not a race

Leave your ego at the start.
No drama, no negativity, no disrespecting other riders
Respect the lead's calls on route and pace
If you have any negative feedback give it in private

Have fun on the trails!
