Community rules

Six rules for everyone in the group.

DisclaimerChiang Mai Enduro Riders is just a group of friends who ride together, not a business, organization, or guide service.We provide no services, and no money changes hands. Ride leaders, admins and other riders are not responsible for your safety or for any legal consequences of the rides. We do not represent any GPS track as safe or legal, and no information shared here is safety or legal advice. Enduro riding is dangerous: you ride at your own risk.
01
Keep it to EnduroThis group is only for organizing rides, discussing dirt bikes, and sharing riding pics.
02
No dramaIf you've got an issue with someone, sort it out in private. Feedback on rides is welcome, but share it privately with organizers or admins.
03
Easy tracks onlyEasy tracks are on our member-only website cnx-enduro.github.io, but do not ask for or share intermediate/harder tracks in the group.Unprepared or irresponsible riders on harder tracks, without more experienced riders to help, can get themselves and the whole CNX Enduro community in trouble with the local authorities. If you want to ride harder tracks, join our organized rides.
04
Don't repost our tracksDo not post any of our community tracks on other websites or social media.
05
No adsNo commercial posts without admin approval.
06
Full gearMinimum gear: helmet, high boots, kneepads, gloves, and goggles on every group ride.

Also, please remember

A
Respect localsRide SLOWLY and SILENTLY through villages and around people and animals.Stay off closed or restricted trails. Be polite and compliant if locals get angry with you for accidental trespassing. Your behavior reflects on the whole Enduro community!
B
Fewer notifications in the chatInstead of replying “lol”, “nice” or “👍”, just react to the message. Try to put your thoughts in one message rather than five short ones, and take one-on-one chats to private messages.

Thank you for the cooperation
