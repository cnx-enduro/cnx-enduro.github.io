Community rules

Chiang Mai Enduro Riders

📜 Rules⚠️ If you break them🙏 Please remember📶 Difficulty levels
1
Keep it to Enduro.This group is only for organizing rides, discussing dirt bikes, and sharing riding pics.
2
No drama.If you've got an issue with someone, sort it out in private. Feedback on rides is welcome, but share it privately with organizers or admins.
3
Easy tracks are on our member-only website cnx-enduro.github.io but do not ask for or share intermediate/harder tracks in the group.Because unprepared or irresponsible people riding harder tracks without the assistance of more experienced riders can get themselves, and the whole CNX enduro community, in trouble with the local authorities. If you want to ride harder tracks, join our organized rides.
4
Do not post any of our community tracks on other websites or social media.
5
No commercial posts without admin approval.

What happens if you break the rules ⚠️

The first timeyou'll get a warning
→
If it happens againyou'll be banned

Also, please remember

🏘️
RESPECT LOCALS: ride SLOWLY and SILENTLY through villages.Stay off closed or restricted trails. Be polite and compliant if locals get angry with you for accidental trespassing. Your behavior reflects on the whole Enduro community!
⚠️
Ride at your own risk:everyone is responsible for themselves and their bike. Admins and leaders are volunteers, not guides, and they do not guarantee your legal or physical safety.
🪖
Minimum gear:helmet, high boots, knee protection, and gloves on every group ride.
🔕
Fewer notifications in the chatInstead of replying “lol”, “nice” or “👍”, just react to the message. Try to put your thoughts in one message rather than five short ones, and take one-on-one chats to private messages.

Thank you for the cooperation🙏
