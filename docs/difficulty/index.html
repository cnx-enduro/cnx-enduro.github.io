Difficulty levels

Every ride is announced with a level. Join rides at or below your level.

L0 PavedL1 EasyL2 NarrowL3 SteepL4 Technical

Rally/ADV: max L1.

Dual sport (CRF): max L3.

50/50 tyres: max L2.

Leader can make exceptions.

Wet: one level harder.

L0

Paved

Sightseeing on concrete or tarmac.

Terrain
Relaxing mountain and village roads.
Skills
Open to all.
Bike
Any.
L1

Easy

Flowing dirt double track or wide single track.

Terrain
Any line, mostly flat, shallow ruts, no obstacles.
Skills
Beginner-friendly, seated riding.
Bike
ADV, CRF, or lighter. 50/50 tyres ok.
L2

Narrow

Flowing single track.

Terrain
Deeper ruts, small up/downhills, some cliff exposure.
Skills
Beginner-friendly, standing riding, slow-speed balance, U-turns, line selection.
Bike
CRF, or lighter. 50/50 tyres ok.
L3

Steep

Slow single track with climbs.

Terrain
Loose and rocky uphills, steep descents, more cliff exposure, small obstacles.
Skills
Clutch control, climb recovery, steep descents, traction control, stamina.
Bike
CRF, or lighter. 100% off-road tyres + rim locks + pull strap.
L4

Technical

Crawl single track with big obstacles.

Terrain
Boulders, root steps, big logs, very steep slopes, heavily overgrown trails.
Skills
Wheel lifts, momentum climbs, walking the bike down, physical fitness.
Bike
< 120 kg. 100% off-road tyres + rim locks + pull strap.
