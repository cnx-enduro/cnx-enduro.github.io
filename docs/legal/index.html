About this group

Chiang Mai Enduro Riders · who we are, and who is responsible for what

DRAFT for the admins to check. Not published.

We are a group of friends who ride together. That is all.This group is a chat of riders who like enduro. It is not an organization, a business, a club with fees, or a guiding service.
✕ not a business✕ no membership fees✕ no paid rides✕ no guiding
1
You are free to come and goMembers are not bound to the group and never pay to be in it. You are free to ride on your own and with other groups, and you can leave the group at any time, for any reason.
2
Riding with this group means following its rulesWhen you ride with this group, you follow the community rules. Ride leaders follow the leader rules.
3
You are responsible for yourselfEvery rider is responsible for their own health, safety, bike, gear and decisions. Ride within your limits. In reality riders look out for each other, but no one takes on responsibility for anyone else.
4
Ride leaders and admins are volunteers, not guidesThey are riders like you who offer to plan a route and set the pace. They do not take responsibility for anyone's health or safety, and they do not guarantee your legal or physical safety.
5
Tracks are shared as they areThe tracks on this site are shared by riders for riders. They are not guaranteed to be safe or up to date, and they are not an official recommendation from the group, its admins or its ride leaders. Trails, weather and conditions change from day to day: check for yourself before and while you ride.
6
Ride legallyYour legal status is your own responsibility: your licence, your bike's papers and insurance, your visa, and following Thai traffic law. Stay off closed and restricted areas, such as national parks.
